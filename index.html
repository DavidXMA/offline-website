The idea

Remember being bored?

Waiting in line. Sitting on the train. Doing absolutely nothing. Somewhere along the way, every empty moment became another reason to check your phone.

We think those moments are worth getting back.

Offline turns time away from distracting apps into something you can see, build on, and even compete over.

01Take control

Your apps. Your rules.

Choose the apps you want to spend less time on. Offline keeps them locked using Apple's Screen Time.

Need to check something? Start a break in Offline. Offline times it, and when your break ends, the app locks again.

No endless scrolling by accident.

5 min→ 10 min→ 15 min

Each break of the day gets a little longer. An offline run of an hour or more sets it back to 5 minutes. You decide when to take them.

Privacy

Your time is yours. So is your data.

Offline uses Apple's Screen Time to lock the apps you choose, not to read your Screen Time activity.

Your app selections stay on your iPhone. Offline measures runs and breaks with its own timer.

If you join a league, other members can see your shared score and ranking. They can't see what you do inside other apps.

No ads. No tracking. No selling your data.

FAQ

Good questions.

More questions?Visit Support
Does Offline actually block my apps?

Yes. Offline uses Apple's Screen Time controls to lock the apps you select. You can temporarily unlock them by starting a break.

Does Offline read my Screen Time activity?

No. Offline doesn't collect Screen Time usage reports. It measures your runs and breaks independently, based on when you start and end them.

What can my friends see?

In a league, members can see your profile, daily score, points, and rank. They cannot see your individual break history or what you do inside other apps.

Can I leave a league or delete my account?

Yes. You can leave leagues or delete your account from the app.

Is Offline really free?

Yes. No subscriptions, ads, or in-app purchases.
