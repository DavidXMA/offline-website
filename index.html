How it works

Breaks, not limits

Your day

Locked by default. A break when you choose one.

  1. 1

    Pick the apps that pull you in

    Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, whatever it is for you. Offline keeps them locked with Apple's Screen Time.

  2. 2

    Take a break when you need one

    Tap the lock screen and the app opens for a set time. Each break of the day lasts a little longer.

    5 min10 min15 min
  3. 3

    A new offline run begins

    When the break is over, the app locks again and the timer starts from zero.

  4. 4

    Your best run counts

    The longest run of your day is your score, and it's what your friends see.
