What does Offline see?

Offline uses Screen Time only to lock and unlock the apps you choose. Usage reports and app identifiers never leave your iPhone. Privacy Policy

What do my friends see in a league?

Your name, photo, daily score, points and rank. Never your break history or what you do inside other apps.

Can I leave or delete my account?

Anytime, in the app: leave a league, or Settings → Delete account.

Does it cost anything?

No. Offline is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
